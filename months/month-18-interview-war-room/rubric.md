## Month 18 Gates

Assessed at the end of the final week, without notes.

### Knowledge gate

- [ ] Solve a medium in 20 minutes with narration, complexity, and a test
- [ ] Derive attention cold in under six minutes
- [ ] Derive backprop for a two-layer MLP cold
- [ ] Deliver any of the twelve system designs in 45 minutes
- [ ] Tell any of the twelve STAR stories in 90-120 seconds
- [ ] Deliver the portfolio walkthrough in four minutes, 90 seconds, and 20 seconds

**6 of 6 required.**

### Implementation gate

- [ ] Nine flagships published to standard
- [ ] All nine setup instructions tested on a clean clone
- [ ] Three demo recordings
- [ ] Six posts published
- [ ] Three full mock loops completed and scored
- [ ] Full loop score 142+/180
- [ ] Applications submitted

**All required.** These are not judgment calls.
