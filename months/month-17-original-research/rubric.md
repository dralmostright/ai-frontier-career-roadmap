## Month 17 Gates

Assessed at the end of the final week, without notes.

### Knowledge gate

- [ ] State a specific, falsifiable, feasible research question
- [ ] State in advance what result would change your mind
- [ ] Defend the experimental design against hostile questioning
- [ ] Report a negative or uncomfortable finding without flinching
- [ ] Present the findings in five minutes to a technical audience

**5 of 5 required.**

### Implementation gate

- [ ] Pre-registration published before running
- [ ] 40+ benchmark scenarios with documented ground truth
- [ ] At least two baselines including a timed human
- [ ] 5+ seeds per condition with CIs reported
- [ ] Failure log with counts, no silent drops
- [ ] **Benchmark released publicly**
- [ ] Report published, capstone scored 9.0+

**All required.** These are not judgment calls.
