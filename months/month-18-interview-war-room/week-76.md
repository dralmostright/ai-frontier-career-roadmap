# Week 76: Mock Interviews and Recruiter Package

## Outcome

By Sunday you have completed three full simulated onsites, scored them, identified the pattern in your weaknesses, and have a resume and outreach materials ready.

## Why This Matters For OpenAI/Anthropic-Level Interviews

**The month's pivot point.** Everything before this prepares for it; everything
after responds to what it exposes.

A full loop is different from individual rounds. Fatigue is real, context
switching is real, and the fifth round is where composure fails. Simulating the
whole thing is the only way to find that out before it costs you.

Score each loop with `coach/interview_rubric.md`. The bar is 142 of 180, with no
round more than one below its threshold. One weak round is survivable; two is not.

Then look for the pattern. Three loops will show you the same weakness three
times, and that is your Week 77-78 work.

## Time Budget: 15-20 Hours

- Theory: 0 hours
- Coding: 4 hours
- Project: 4 hours
- Interview practice: 10 hours
- Review/write-up: 3 hours

## Theory Lessons

1. **The full loop**
   1. Four to five rounds: coding, ML theory, DL depth, system design, behavioral
   2. Fatigue and context switching
   3. Composure in round five
2. **Scoring**
   1. `coach/interview_rubric.md`, all five tracks
   2. 142/180 as the bar
   3. Two rounds below threshold is a fail
3. **The resume**
   1. One page
   2. Flagships with numbers
   3. Positioning line at the top
   4. Background as advantage
4. **Outreach**
   1. Referral requests
   2. Cold outreach to hiring managers
   3. Recruiter responses

## Hands-On Exercises

```bash
cd bootstrap/environment && make week W=76
```

1. ****Loop 1**** (3h) — Five rounds, recorded, minimal breaks. Score it.
2. **Fix one thing** (1.5h) — Exactly one weakness from loop 1.
3. ****Loop 2**** (3h) — Different questions. Score it.
4. **Fix one thing** (1.5h) — One.
5. ****Loop 3**** (3h) — Score it. Compare all three.
6. **Identify the pattern** (1h) — What failed in all three? That is Week 77-78's work.
7. **The resume** (2h) — One page. Numbers on every flagship. Positioning line at the top.
8. **Outreach templates** (1.5h) — Referral request, cold hiring-manager message, recruiter response. Three each.
9. **The twelve STAR stories** (2h) — Final polish. 90-120 seconds each, recorded.

## Stretch Goal

Do a fourth loop with real partners rather than self-administered — interviewing.io, or colleagues who will take it seriously. Self-administered mocks are calibrated by you, which means they are calibrated optimistically. An outside score on a full loop is the single most reliable readiness signal available.
