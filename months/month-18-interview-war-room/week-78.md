# Week 78: Final Championship Review

## Outcome

By Sunday you have an honest hire/no-hire self-assessment backed by evidence, a plan for any remaining gaps, and a sustainable routine for the interview period ahead.

## Why This Matters For OpenAI/Anthropic-Level Interviews

The course ends with an honest assessment rather than a celebration.

Score the full readiness rubric. Compare against the Month 1 baseline — that
comparison is worth seeing, because eighteen months of steady work is hard to
perceive from the inside.

Then make the call. Below 25 on the interview readiness score means extending
rather than pushing forward with applications; applying underprepared burns
referrals and companies for six to twelve months. Above 31 means apply broadly
and quickly, while you are sharp.

Whatever the number, set up the maintenance routine. Interview processes take
weeks and skills decay. A few hours a week of drills and one technical
conversation a week keeps you at the level you have reached.

## Evaluation Rubric

| Score | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| 3 | Barely started. Tests failing. |
| 5 | Tests pass. Cannot explain the mechanism. |
| 7 | All tests pass. Clean code. Can explain every choice. Artifact committed. |
| 9 | Above, plus the stretch analysis done and the interview drill answered cold. |
| 10 | Above, plus you found and fixed something the tests did not catch. |

## Stretch Goal

Publish the retrospective. Eighteen months of documented, structured work moving from database engineering into AI is a story people want to read, and it is the best possible distribution channel for the portfolio you just spent a year and a half building. Lead with what did not work — those are the parts that make it useful rather than promotional.
