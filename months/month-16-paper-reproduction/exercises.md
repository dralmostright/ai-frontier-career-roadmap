# Month 16 Exercises

Every exercise in one place. The **core** set is required — it is what the tests
check. **Extensions** are optional, ordered by value.

---

## If You Finish Early

Priority: Week 63's transfer test to your domain data (the bridge to Month 17), Week 62's author contact, Week 61's OpenReview calibration. Also: start thinking about the Month 17 research question now. A good question takes time to find.
